# YouTube Short Script — "Yahoo Killed My App Overnight"

Based on the blog post: *Public APIs Continued To Be Walled Off*
Target length: ~50 seconds. Talking-head style, fast cuts, text overlays on every beat.

---

## Title options (pick one)

1. Yahoo killed my app overnight 💀
2. My free app broke — and it's AI's fault
3. Big Tech is quietly locking devs out

## The script

**[0:00–0:03] HOOK — say it cold, no intro**

> Yahoo just broke my app overnight — and I didn't do anything wrong.

*Overlay: "Yahoo broke my app 💀" — cut on every sentence from here on.*

**[0:03–0:12] THE STORY**

> Two years ago I built FFAwards — a free tool that makes awards for your fantasy football league. No ads, no subscriptions. Just a fun thing for the community.

*B-roll: screen recording of FFAwards.app UI.*

**[0:12–0:22] THE TWIST**

> Then this year, Yahoo changed the rules. You can't just register your app anymore — you have to *apply* for API access... and wait weeks or months for a reply. Meanwhile? They revoked access to existing apps. Mine included.

*Overlay: "Access: REVOKED ❌"*

**[0:22–0:33] THE JAW-DROP DETAIL**

> And if you DO get approved, you sign a contract that says any AI output built on their data is **owned by Yahoo** — and they can audit your tech at any time. For a side project that makes zero dollars.

*Overlay: "owned by YAHOO 🤯" — pause half a beat here, let it land.*

**[0:33–0:43] THE WHY**

> And honestly... I get it. AI scrapers are hammering these platforms. Reddit did this in 2023. Strava did it too. The era of open APIs built on trust is over.

*Overlay: "Reddit ✅ Strava ✅ Yahoo ✅"*

**[0:43–0:50] PUNCHLINE + CTA**

> But when you build your house on someone else's land, they can bulldoze it whenever they want. Hobbyists are the ones caught in the crossfire.
>
> Has an API change ever killed YOUR project? Tell me in the comments.

*Overlay: "Never build on rented land 🏠" → end card.*

## Why this structure gets views

- **Hook in under 3 seconds** with a personal loss ("broke my app overnight") — retention lives or dies here.
- **Curiosity gap:** "and I didn't do anything wrong" forces the viewer to wait for the reason.
- **The "owned by Yahoo" contract clause** is the shareable jaw-drop moment — people comment and share outrage.
- **Naming Reddit + Strava** widens the audience beyond fantasy football to all of tech.
- **Question CTA** ("has an API change killed your project?") drives comments, which drives the algorithm.
